Golfed Support

Welcome to the support page for Golfed.

Golfed helps you track your rounds, discover courses, and connect with other golfers through your account and profile.

Need Help?

If you are experiencing issues, have questions, or want to report a bug, please contact support at oscarroos123@gmail.com.

Common Issues

Login issues: Verify your email and password, then try again. If needed, use password reset in the app.

Upload problems: Check your internet connection and try again.

Found a bug? Please email a short description of the issue and, if possible, include screenshots and your device model.

Feedback

Suggestions for improvements and new features are always welcome.
