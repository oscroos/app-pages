Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Golfed handles information.

Information We Collect

Golfed may collect account-related information required to provide login and core app functionality, such as your email address, username, and profile details.

When you use sharing features, content you upload or submit in the app may be processed and stored to provide the service.

How We Use Information

Information is used to create and manage your account, authenticate logins, provide app features, maintain security, and improve app performance.

Analytics and Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party analytics or similar services to understand basic app usage, improve performance, and enhance the user experience.

These services may collect limited technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, and general usage data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Support Requests

If you contact support by email, any information you provide, such as your email address and the contents of your message, will be used only to respond to your request.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by third-party service providers only as needed to operate, secure, analyze, or improve the app.

Data Retention

Account and service-related data may be retained while your account is active and for a limited period afterward as needed for legal, security, and operational purposes.

Children's Privacy

Golfed is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
